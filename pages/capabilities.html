Capabilities

What we can actually do.

Analysis is useful only when it leads somewhere. The work is designed to produce clearer decisions, better workflows, stronger operational visibility, or a useful thing that did not exist before.

Operational Analysis

Examine how work moves through an organization, identify friction and hidden dependencies, and translate observations into practical changes. Typical outputs include workflow analysis, process documentation, labor and cost models, operational recommendations, and decision-support frameworks.

Business Discovery

Build a structured picture of a business from public information, customer experience, competitive context, operational signals, and available evidence. The objective is understanding, not a decorative company profile.

Hospitality Operations

Analyze service execution, banquet operations, guest experience, food and labor considerations, training, readiness, and the gap between event information and what frontline teams actually need to execute.

AI-Assisted Analysis

Use AI to organize information, surface patterns, accelerate research, support structured analysis, and create practical decision-support tools while keeping human review where judgment matters.

Workflow & Process Design

Turn informal knowledge and inconsistent execution into repeatable workflows, checklists, documentation, operating views, and practical standards.

Technology & Systems Thinking

Evaluate technology as part of a larger operating system. The question is not simply what a tool can do. It is whether the surrounding workflow, people, information, and constraints make the tool useful.

Capability Intelligence

Structure what a person, team, or organization can actually do, including evidence, maturity, relationships between capabilities, and how that information can support decisions.

A distinction that matters

Demonstrated. Developing. Conceptual.

Not every idea is a finished product. The Looking Crass distinguishes between work already demonstrated, capabilities actively being developed, and concepts being explored. Credibility improves when those categories stay separate.
